SECTION 4: NATURAL CALAMITY & FLOOD COVERAGE
Clause 4.2 (Flood Coverage): Flood damage to the insured vehicle resulting from heavy rainfall, waterlogging, or flash flooding is covered under this policy (claim_type: flood_damage).
Clause 4.7 (Waiting Period): Flood damage claims are payable only if the loss occurs 30 or more days after the policy start date (days_since_policy_start >= 30). Any flood loss occurring within the first 29 days of policy inception is excluded.

SECTION 5: LIMITS, DEDUCTIBLES & SETTLEMENT
Clause 5.1 (Maximum Coverage Limit): The maximum coverage limit payable for any single flood damage claim under this policy is INR 150000 (claim_amount <= 150000).
Clause 5.2 (Compulsory Deductible): A standard deductible of INR 10000 applies to every approved flood damage claim prior to final settlement.
Clause 5.3 (Documentation): All claims must be accompanied by an authorized service center repair estimate and proof of incident date.
